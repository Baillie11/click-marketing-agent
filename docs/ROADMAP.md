# Product roadmap

## V0.1 — Internal Prototype (implemented)

Authentication, business profiles and switching, dashboard, crawler, page inventory, migrations, and seed data.

## V0.2 — Content Workspace (implemented baseline)

Content library, statuses, calendar/list scheduling, brand context, and article/social draft generation.

## V0.3 — Website & SEO Audit (implemented baseline)

Latest-audit reporting, prior-audit comparison, audit history, actionable content suggestions, platform-specific implementation guidance, overlapping-crawl prevention, automation preferences, and guarded change proposals are implemented. Live WordPress/Shopify publishing remains locked pending verified token-based connectors, field mapping, conflict detection, verification, and rollback.

Technical/on-page checks, transparent health score, persisted issues, and report interface. Next: duplicate-content clustering, link status checks, content opportunities, before/after editor.

## V1.0 — Marketing MVP (current consolidation target)

Harden background crawling, complete recommendation creation from audits, expand UI editing/approval, add marketing-plan draft creation, integration tests, password reset, and production observability.
