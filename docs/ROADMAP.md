# Product roadmap

## V0.1 — Internal Prototype (implemented)

Authentication, business profiles and switching, dashboard, crawler, page inventory, migrations, and seed data.

## V0.2 — Content Workspace (implemented baseline)

Content library, statuses, calendar/list scheduling, brand context, and article/social draft generation.

## V0.3 — Website & SEO Audit (implemented baseline)

Latest-audit reporting, prior-audit comparison, audit history, AI visual-consistency review from desktop/mobile screenshots, actionable content suggestions, platform-specific implementation guidance, overlapping-crawl prevention, automation preferences, guarded change proposals, and an encrypted WordPress Application Password connection flow are implemented. Live field publishing and Shopify connections remain locked pending field mapping, conflict detection, post-write verification, and rollback.

Technical/on-page checks, transparent health score, persisted issues, and report interface. Next: duplicate-content clustering, link status checks, content opportunities, before/after editor.

## V1.0 — Marketing MVP (current consolidation target)

Controlled staging automation is available for WordPress development copies: the live site remains the audit source, a separate staging URL is verified, localhost is permitted only in development, and automated writes are rejected unless the connection and prepared change both target staging. Each supported change performs conflict detection and post-write verification. Account creation, first-business onboarding, secure password recovery, owner-managed users, structured error responses, dependency security checks, liveness/readiness endpoints and production deployment guidance are implemented.

Deferred beyond the V1 launch boundary: live-site writes, rollback automation, durable background job queues, duplicate-content clustering, Google Search Console/ranking integrations, marketing-plan generation, social publishing, analytics and paid advertising. These remain deliberately unavailable rather than appearing as non-functional controls.

### Lead Opportunities MVP (implemented)

Manual public-post capture, platform-aware URL deduplication, business outreach policies, AI-assisted or manual reply drafting, immutable revisions, role-controlled review, exact-revision approval, manual-publication evidence and transactional audit history are implemented. Approval never posts externally. Permitted discovery connectors and authorised social execution remain later milestones.
